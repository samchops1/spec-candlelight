01 · Who he is

A Denver bartender, not a brand

Scott Hanley works the stick at Candlelight Tavern on South Pearl. Weekends, people show up for him and for Chris Johnson — same long shift, same room. Sit there long enough and you’ve heard his name before you put a face to it.

He’s not selling a cocktail brand online. He’s the guy who’s been there. Dishwasher, busboy, host, waiter, then bartender. He puts the career at about 43 years, including a stretch in L.A. before Denver.

02 · The bar

Candlelight Tavern

Wash Park dive. Neon out front. Cheap drinks, burgers, wings, pool and darts inside. Lisa and Dave Bryan have owned it since 1997. This is Scott’s home base.

People argue dive vs neighborhood bar. On the podcasts Scott and Chris shrug at the dive label and talk about late nights to 2 a.m., industry regulars who followed Scott from older stops, and the holiday potlucks Dave and Lisa put on for anyone in the neighborhood who needs a seat. Westword named it Best Dive in 2020. The room still feels like a bar that knows your name.

03 · The years

How he got here

  1. 1992

    Scott meets Chris

    They start working together at the Himalaya in Aurora. Cricket and the Palm come later. More than thirty years side by side before anyone called them a pairing.

  2. ~1997–98

    Candlelight

    Scott hung out on Pearl before Dave bought the place. He dates starting behind the stick to 1998, recruited from the Palm downtown. Chris lands a few years after — about 22 years at Candlelight by late 2025. Scott’s going on 27.

  3. 2017

    “A legend”

    Westword sits with the Bryans for a dive-bar feature. Lisa doesn’t hedge: Scott is “a legend.” Not a flyer line. The people who own the place.

  4. Still

    Same stick

    Same owners. Same partner. Regulars who’ve been coming long enough that their kids — and grandkids — know the room. Industry friends still walk in after shifts.

04 · 2018

When Scott got sick, people showed up

In 2018 Scott was diagnosed with cancer. Friends ran a GoFundMe. Candlelight threw a fundraiser night. Kentucky Inn and other bars put on a pub crawl. He took shifts off, got through treatment, and came back to the stick.

His Facebook lines up with that year: Denver, Candlelight, birthday on August 30, the fundraisers.

05 · 2025

HOSPY Bartender of the Year

On November 10, 2025, the Colorado Restaurant Association named Scott the 2025 HOSPY Bartender of the Year. That fall, on a Laxin’ episode, he’d shrugged about the nomination — he hadn’t even known he was in it. The award caught up to what Wash Park already said out loud.
