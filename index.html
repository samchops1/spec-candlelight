01 · Who he is

A Denver bartender, not a brand

Scott Hanley works behind the bar at Candlelight Tavern on South Pearl. On weekends, people show up for him and for Chris Johnson. They share the same long shift in the same room. Sit there long enough and you have heard his name before you put a face to it.

He is not selling a cocktail brand online. He is the guy who has been there. He worked as a dishwasher, busboy, host, and waiter before he became a bartender. He puts the career at about forty-three years, including a stretch in Los Angeles before Denver.

02 · The bar

Candlelight Tavern

Candlelight is a Wash Park dive with neon out front. Inside you find cheap drinks, burgers, wings, pool, and darts. Lisa and Dave Bryan have owned it since 1997. This is Scott’s home base.

People still argue about whether it is a dive or a neighborhood bar. On the podcasts, Scott and Chris shrug at the dive label. They talk about late nights until two in the morning, industry regulars who followed Scott from older stops, and the holiday potlucks Dave and Lisa put on for anyone in the neighborhood who needs a seat. Westword named it Best Dive in 2020. The room still feels like a bar that knows your name.

03 · The years

How he got here

1992. Scott meets Chris. They start working together at the Himalaya in Aurora. Cricket and the Palm come later. More than thirty years side by side pass before anyone calls them a pairing.

Around 1997 to 1998. Candlelight. Scott hung out on Pearl before Dave bought the place. He dates starting behind this bar to 1998, after he was recruited from the Palm downtown. Chris lands a few years after. By late 2025 Chris has about twenty-two years at Candlelight. Scott is going on twenty-seven.

2017. A legend. Westword sits with the Bryans for a dive-bar feature. Lisa does not hedge. She says Scott is “a legend.” It is not a flyer line. It comes from the people who own the place.

Still. Same bar. Same owners. Same partner. Regulars have been coming long enough that their kids, and grandkids, know the room. Industry friends still walk in after shifts.

04 · 2018

When Scott got sick, people showed up

In 2018, Scott was diagnosed with cancer. Friends ran a GoFundMe. Candlelight threw a fundraiser night. Kentucky Inn and other bars put on a pub crawl. He took shifts off, got through treatment, and came back behind the bar.

05 · 2025

HOSPY Bartender of the Year

On November 10, 2025, the Colorado Restaurant Association named Scott the 2025 HOSPY Bartender of the Year. That fall, on a Laxin’ episode, he had shrugged about the nomination. He had not even known he was in it. The award caught up to what Wash Park already said out loud.

07 · On the mic

Hear him talk

Three full episodes with Scott and Chris sit below. The tenure dates and bar stories on this page come from these recordings, not from a press kit.

Who The Fuck Closed Last Night? · Season 1, Episode 17

At Candlelight with Chad and Jacob

October 13, 2025 · 1 hour 6 minutes · Recorded on Pearl

The mics are in the tavern on Pearl. Scott and Chris spend the hour on how they got here. They start in Aurora in 1992, move through a few downtown rooms, then spend decades under Dave and Lisa. What sticks is the late nights, the regulars who followed Scott, and the holiday potlucks the Bryans still throw for whoever needs a seat. They will not sell Candlelight as a dive. Neighborhood bar is the word they keep using.

“I’ve been here going on 27 years now.” Scott

Laxin’ at the Lair · Episode 43

Scott and Chris at Lion’s Lair

October 23, 2025 · 1 hour 26 minutes · Chuck Gibson and Travis Dowdy

Chuck and Travis pull them over to Lion’s Lair for a long hang. Scott dates the Pearl bar to 1998. They remember pajama Sundays before the Bryans bought the place, then a quieter room with fewer fights and industry friends walking people to cars. Somebody mentions bartender of the year. Scott shrugs. He had not even known he was nominated. Chuck closes by calling him one of the legendary bartenders in the city.

Scott, Chris, and Chad (Fuck Yes Tequila)
