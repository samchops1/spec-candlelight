Behind the stick

A Denver bartender’s story

01

Longtime Candlelight

Scott Hanley is a longtime bartender at Candlelight Tavern on South Pearl. He works alongside Chris Johnson — podcasts put the two of them at roughly 22 years together behind the stick. Weekends, regulars come for Scott and Chris.

02

Called a legend

In a 2017 Westword dive-bar feature, co-owner Lisa Bryan put it plainly: Scott is “a legend.” Not a slogan from a flyer — the people who own the place, saying what the stools already knew.

03

The neighborhood showed up

In 2018, when Scott faced cancer, the community rallied around him. He’s still behind the stick — pouring, joking, making Wash Park feel like home.

04

2025 HOSPY Bartender of the Year

On November 10, 2025, the Colorado Restaurant Association named Scott the 2025 HOSPY Bartender of the Year. A career of nights at 383 S Pearl, recognized statewide.

Who The Fuck Closed Last Night? · S1E17

CandleLight Tavern with Scott and Chris

Oct 13, 2025 · 1:06:26 · Hosts Chad Halbrook & Jacob Stone

Listen on RSS.com

Recorded at Candlelight on Pearl, Chad and Jacob sit with Scott and Chris — the hosts’ Denver service-industry legends. Scott puts Candlelight at going on 27 years; Chris at about 22. They’ve worked together since 1992 (Himalaya in Aurora), then Cricket and the Palm, before landing under owners Dave and Lisa Bryan. Scott hung out on Pearl before Dave bought the place and was recruited from the Palm downtown.

They talk industry nights to 2 a.m., regulars who followed Scott from earlier bars, engagements and receptions in the room, and holiday potluck meals Dave and Lisa put on for anyone in the neighborhood who needs a seat. “Dive” vs neighborhood bar comes up; they lean neighborhood. One of them marks 43 years behind sticks, including a stretch in L.A. before Denver.

  • “I’ve been here going on 27 years now.” — Scott Hanley
  • “I started here probably about 22 years ago.” — Chris Johnson
  • “We started working together in ’92… over 30 years ago… the Himalaya in Aurora.” — Scott & Chris
  • “I’ll be bartending for 43 years… dishwasher, busboy, host, waiter, and then started bartending.” — Scott Hanley
  • “Dave and Lisa have made sure… three holidays… free potluck for anybody in the neighborhood… Thanksgiving, Christmas, Easter… the community notices that.” — Scott / Chris
  • “That’s a cocktail you can’t make.” — on the room’s community vibe
  • Hosts: Candlelight as “the quintessential Denver bar.”

Laxin’ at the Lair · YouTube Episode 43

Chris Johnson and Scott Hanley (Candlelight Tavern)

Oct 23, 2025 · 1:26:38 · Hosts Chuck Gibson & Travis Dowdy · Lion’s Lair

Watch on YouTube · RSS

At Lion’s Lair, Chuck and Travis dig into Colfax history and how Scott and Chris’s friendship predates Candlelight (they met around 1992–93). Scott dates starting at Candlelight to 1998, working for Dave and Lisa Bryan. Hosts note Scott is up for bartender of the year with the Colorado Restaurant Association — he says he didn’t even know he’d been nominated.

They chart the bar’s arc from a rougher “super dive” hang (Sunday mornings in pajamas before the Bryans bought it) to a safer neighborhood spot where industry friends show up and people get walked to their cars. Softballs and Dave buying a beaten opponent a drink is how one of them first walked in. Walk-ins still discover the place after years of passing by. Closing shout: Scott as one of the city’s legendary bartenders.

  • “I started [at] the Candlelight in ’98.” — Scott Hanley
  • “We work for Dave and Lisa Bryan… We’ve been working for them for a long time.” — Scott / Chris
  • “Scott is up for bartender of the year… He didn’t even know he was nominated — the Colorado Restaurant Association.” — hosts
  • “Candlelight is a place for good people.” — host framing
  • “I wouldn’t even necessarily say it’s a dive bar anymore, but it still has that dive [feel].” — Scott / Chris
  • “We used to hang out there way before we worked there… before Dave and Lisa Bryan bought the bar.” — Scott / Chris
  • “One of those legendary bartenders in the whole city as well.” — host, closing

Laxin’ at the Lair · Chuck’s Birthday (PitchCentric #79)

Chris & Scott + Chad (Fuck Yes Tequila)

Sep 3, 2026 · ~1:25 · Hosts Chuck Gibson & Travis Dowdy · Lion’s Lair

Watch on YouTube · RSS

Birthday special for Chuck with Travis, Scott, Chris, and Chad of Fuck Yes Tequila. Cold open: Scott, Chris, and Chuck stack a collective 111 years of bartending experience. Chris jokes he’s been “married to Scott longer than my wife.” Chad ties the tequila brand’s Denver start to Candlelight — first shots, friendship, and gratitude for the stick that moved the bottles.

Much of the middle is Denver scene hang (tips, cash, bands, birthday week). When burgers come up, the room lands on Candlelight as a top-three burger in the city. Less structured interview than the other two episodes — more proof of how deep Scott and Chris sit in the service-industry social graph.

  • “I’m Scott Hanley, Candlelight…” — Scott Hanley
  • “The three of us have 111 years… experience, because last year was 108.” — Scott / Chris / Chuck
  • “I’ve actually been married to Scott longer than my wife.” — Chris Johnson
  • “I met Chad at the Candlelight… that’s when I had my first shot of Fuck Yes.” — on Candlelight × Fuck Yes Tequila
  • “Candlelight is like top three burgers in the whole city.” — guest / host
